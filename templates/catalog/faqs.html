A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, then send your order enquiry to our team on WhatsApp. We will confirm availability, delivery, and payment details with you.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

We arrange delivery across Kenya. Delivery options, cost, and estimated timing depend on your location and the product; our team will confirm these before finalizing your order.

Can I ask for help choosing a product?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us on +254 700 007 552, or email info@qiksearch.co.ke.

How can I pay?

Our team will confirm the available payment methods when confirming your order. Please wait for official payment instructions from Qiksearch.

How do I ask about a return?

Contact us with your order details before returning a product. See our Returns and Refunds Policy for more information.
