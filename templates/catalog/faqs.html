How do I place an order? ▾

Add products to your cart, then send your order enquiry to our team directly on WhatsApp. We will confirm availability, delivery cost to your county, and payment details with you.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi? ▾

Yes, we arrange reliable delivery across all 47 counties in Kenya. Delivery options, cost, and estimated timing depend on your location and the product; our team will confirm these before finalizing your order.

Can I ask for help choosing the right equipment? ▾

Absolutely! Call or WhatsApp us on +254 700 007 552, or email info@qiksearch.co.ke. Our team has in-house agricultural expertise to help you make the right choice for your acreage and soil.

How can I make payments? ▾

Our team will provide official payment details (such as M-Pesa Till / Paybill or Bank Transfer) when confirming your order items.

How do I request a return or warranty inquiry? ▾

Contact us with your order reference before returning any product. See our Returns and Refunds Policy for detailed terms.
