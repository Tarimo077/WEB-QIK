WHAT WE REALLY DO

Better tools.
Stronger farms.

Qiksearch is a Kenyan social enterprise dedicated to enhancing the livelihoods and resilience of agricultural communities by improving farming productivity, minimizing pre- and post-harvest losses, and addressing climate change in arid and semi-arid regions.

Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Nairobi, Qiksearch designs, manufactures and distributes affordable, user-friendly agricultural tools and equipment. We also supply pasture seeds, provide farmer training and create market value-chain linkages.
